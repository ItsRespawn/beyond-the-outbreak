SUPPORTER PROGRAM.

Beyond the Outbreak is a community-driven DayZ server project. This page describes our community supporter benefits, server-wide donation rewards, exclusive cosmetic items and reserved-slot options.

PROGRAM INFORMATION: Beyond the Outbreak reports approval of its community-wide donation payout plan. Availability of specific cosmetics, reserved slots and payment checkout is confirmed separately by staff. This website does not itself process payments or automatically distribute rewards.

01 / Objective

KEEP THE SERVERS RUNNING.

Funds received through an approved supporter program would be used to help cover the cost of game server hosting, maintenance and backups, technical operations, and community development. Participating would be entirely optional for players on our free-access servers. Everyone would continue to be subject to the same community rules and administrative standards.

03 / Community-wide donation payouts

EVERY PLAYER RECEIVES.

Each qualifying individual donation unlocks a same-sized in-game Hryvnia payout for every eligible player. The operator reports that this specific server-wide payout plan was approved. Staff confirm recipient eligibility and distribute payments.

Individual donation

$25 · $50 · $100 · $200

Each eligible player receives

100,000 · 250,000 · 500,000 · 1,000,000 Hryvnia

Separate transactions, separate payouts. Two $25 donations mean two 100,000 Hryvnia payments, not one 250,000 payment. Payouts do not expire or reset because another person donates.

04 / Reserved server queue slots

$10 PER CALENDAR MONTH.

Queue reservations are available by staff confirmation and limited by the server's configured capacity. A reserved slot improves queue priority but never kicks an active player or increases total server capacity.

Monthly fairness rule: Reservations expire at the end of the purchased calendar month, never renew automatically, and return to the available pool for new applicants. Former holders may apply again. One reserved entitlement per player account.

03 / Community Protection

NO PAY-TO-WIN. NO EXCEPTIONS.

What we will NOT sell

  • Individual purchases of weapons, ammunition or supplies
  • Vehicles, buildings, property, bases, or extra storage
  • Skill boosts, increased stats, or better loot chances
  • Individual trader discounts, economy advantages or special spawn gear outside the shared community payout plan
  • Special treatment from staff or exemptions from rules

Equal player experience

  • Standard access to the server is free
  • No gameplay systems are locked behind supporter tiers
  • Supporters and non-supporters follow identical rules
  • Staff support and tickets remain available to everyone
  • Cosmetic recognition does not alter game balance

We will respect the specific terms of our approval and third-party mod permissions. The approved equal community Hryvnia payout is distinguished from individual currency purchases; the latter are not offered.

04 / Transparency

PAYMENTS & PLAYER TERMS.

No checkout, payment provider, or subscription service is connected to this website at this time. Before any purchase through an approved external payment provider, staff will display the price, coverage dates, fulfillment terms, cancellation instructions, contact options and refund policy. Reserved-slot reservations do not automatically renew.

Supporter recognition will be optional. No contributor will be asked to disclose personal information publicly. We will provide support for billing questions through our official Discord Ticket Tool process.

Voluntary gifts that provide no benefit in return remain separate from supporter packages and donations that trigger rewards. Terms of each option must be clearly stated before payment.
