RESERVED SERVER SLOT.

Optional reserved queue priority on a supported server. No player is kicked for a reservation; the server's maximum capacity remains the same.

Availability: Staff-confirmed only. Request a slot through a support ticket.

Request a Reserved Slot →

HOW THE MONTHLY RESET WORKS

  1. A reserved slot lasts for the calendar month stated when purchased.
  2. All reservations expire at the end of that month. No automatic renewals or recurring charges.
  3. Available reservations are offered again each month so other players have a chance.
  4. Previous holders may reapply; staff prioritize a fair allocation while capacity is limited.
  5. One reservation per player account; normal community rules still apply.

Reservations are staff-managed and require server-side reserved-slot configuration before activation.

Support program policies

KEEP IT FAIR.
KEEP IT OPTIONAL.

Beyond the Outbreak provides optional supporter recognition, server-wide Hryvnia donation rewards, reserved queue priority and cosmetic-only appearance items under the community's stated approved monetization arrangement. The server-wide payout is shared equally; individual cosmetic packages have no special gameplay stats. A reserved slot does not add server capacity or grant staff privileges.

Donations with no benefit are separate from donations that trigger rewards. All members are held to the same gameplay and conduct rules. Server-specific conditions and applicable mod-author permissions continue to apply.

Visit the full monetization program for prices and complete details. For a purchase or fulfillment question, contact the staff through Discord tickets.

Support questions

FREQUENTLY ASKED
QUESTIONS.

Do I have to donate to play?

No. Everyone can join the community's public PvE servers without purchasing anything.

How do the Hryvnia donation rewards work?

Each qualifying contribution is treated separately. A $25 donation provides 100,000 Hryvnia to each eligible player, twice means two separate payouts, not one $50 payout. Staff will publish fulfillment information.

Is a reserved slot included with supporter recognition?

No. Reserved slots are a separate optional $10-per-month offering, subject to availability.

Can I renew a reserved slot?

There is no automatic renewal. Slots return to the pool every month so new players can apply. Existing holders can request another month if space remains.

Are the cosmetic items ready?

Not yet. Prices are listed so players know the planned options. The site will show real images when the gear, clothing and weapon textures are completed.

How do I contact staff?

Use the official Discord support ticket panel linked from Staff & Support.
